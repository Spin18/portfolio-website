## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does a typical project start?**

Every project begins with a free discovery call. I'll review your homepage or shop on the spot, give you three actionable tips, and we'll figure out together whether there's a fit. If there is, you'll get a clear proposal with scope, timeline, and pricing within a few days.

**How long does a project take?**

A UX/CRO audit typically takes 2–4 weeks. A landing page redesign can be live within a week. Full website and shop builds run 4–8 weeks depending on scope. I'll give you a realistic timeline in the proposal, and flag it early if anything shifts.

**Can you just fix things instead of doing a full audit?**

Yes, I offer a Quick Win Sprint where I identify and implement the top conversion fixes directly in your site.

**What platforms do you work with?**

Webflow, Shopify, Framer, WordPress, Squarespace, and Wix. I'll recommend the platform that fits your team's technical comfort and long-term needs, not the one I prefer.

**How much does it cost?**

It depends on scope. Audits start from a fixed price; design and build projects are scoped individually. You'll always get a clear quote before we start. Payment is split into three milestones: 30% upfront, 40% at interim review, 30% on delivery.

**Do you work remotely?**

Yes. I'm based in Berlin and work with clients internationally. Communication runs through email, video calls, and shared Figma files. Timezone overlaps have never been a problem.

**Who do you work best with?**

**What happens after launch?**

I hand over everything your team needs to maintain the site independently: documentation, logins, and a walkthrough if needed. If you want ongoing optimisation or A/B testing support, we can set that up too.
