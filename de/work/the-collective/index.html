Case Study

The Collective

From Directory to Community Platform

Cover image placeholder
Role
UX, CRO & Digital Strategy Consultant
Timeline
4 weeks
Focus
Strategic Direction, Conversion, UX, SEO & AI Visibility
Tools
GA4, Ahrefs, Crazy Egg, Lighthouse, ChatGPT (AI visibility testing), Google Rich Results, Figma, WordPress

A decade-old wellness directory in Bali with 885 referring domains, Wikipedia citations on its cultural content, and a position in 165 Google AI Overviews — operating within a three-property ecosystem that included a physical yoga studio and an annual wellness festival under the same ownership. The site had significant domain authority and editorial assets, but no defined strategic role within the ecosystem. The brief: assess the site's current state, define a strategic direction for the platform, and deliver a conversion-ready roadmap that connects it to the broader ecosystem.

The opportunity

The site's editorial content was quietly outperforming its directory function — three of the top five pages by traffic were blog articles, not listings. A single ceremony page had earned backlinks from three Wikipedia articles. The retreat and accommodation directories showed the longest session durations on the site (above one minute), confirming visitor intent to compare and decide. This was a platform with proven editorial authority and engaged audiences, ready for a strategic model that could channel that attention into something more purposeful. The competitor landscape revealed white space: established publishers owned the content lane, booking platforms owned the transaction lane, but no one held the community position — connecting practitioners, travellers, and locals year-round.

The approach

Stakeholder interview covering site history, team capabilities, competitor positioning, CMS constraints, and ecosystem dynamics. Strategic direction analysis evaluating four alternatives (tourist guide, cultural storytelling, curated marketplace, community platform) against current assets and available resources. UX/CRO walkthroughs of the five highest-traffic pages with heuristic scoring, annotated wireframes, and Crazy Egg behavioural validation. Full technical audit including Core Web Vitals, site health scoring, and CMS migration assessment. Backlink profile analysis and anchor text quality review. SEO keyword analysis confirming cultural content as the site's ranking strength over commercial terms. AI visibility testing across four commercial queries, mapping the gap between the site's existing AI citations (cultural content) and the queries the new strategic direction would need to own. Rebrand and visual direction with a shared colour system across the three-property ecosystem.

What shipped

A 50-page audit document with approximately 40 PIE-scored findings, a phased roadmap across five phases (direction decision, foundation launch, community growth, monetisation, ecosystem maturity), and annotated Figma wireframes for all five flows. The centrepiece was a strategic direction recommendation: reposition the site as a curated community platform anchored by a WhatsApp Community, a newsletter, and a member directory — with a website application form gating entry and ecosystem integration giving members tangible benefits across all three properties. The roadmap included a CMS migration plan with plugin stack, a tiered redirect strategy protecting the site's highest-value URLs, a community onboarding flow, and a monetisation model built on promoted listings and paid membership tiers. Visual direction established a shared terracotta colour system across the ecosystem, with distinct accent colours differentiating each property. The naming recommendation gave the community a verbal identity designed for belonging rather than consumption.

The impact

The audit reframed the internal conversation from "what do we do with this site" to "this site is the connective tissue of the ecosystem." The strategic direction gave the client a decision framework — three specific Phase 0 decisions — rather than an open-ended list of improvements. The redesign and visual unification unlocked the highest-value SEO opportunity available: ecosystem cross-linking between two high-authority domains that no amount of external link building could replicate. The project manager's immediate response to the community model: "Love the community ideas" — followed by tactical questions about implementation, confirming the direction landed as actionable rather than aspirational.
