About

Hi, I'm Imen.

I'm a UX, CRO, and digital strategy consultant based in Berlin. I work with e-commerce, wellness, and SaaS brands that know their product is strong but suspect their website is leaving money on the table. My job is to find out where and fix it — through conversion audits, UX redesigns, landing page builds, and structured A/B testing.

I've spent 10+ years moving between corporate innovation and independent work. At Deloitte, Siemens, and BMW I learned how large organisations approach product, research, and scale. On my own, I learned how to ship fast, prioritise ruthlessly, and make every recommendation something a small team can actually implement. I bring both.

My background is technical — IT and Electrical Engineering at TU Munich — and I worked in neuromarketing at the Deloitte Neuroscience Institute, which means I don't just look at heatmaps and analytics. I understand how attention, trust, and decision-making work at a cognitive level, and I design for that.

Fluent in German, English, French, and Arabic. Based in Berlin, working internationally.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How does a typical project start?

Every project begins with a free discovery call. I'll review your homepage or shop on the spot, give you three actionable tips, and we'll figure out together whether there's a fit. If there is, you'll get a clear proposal with scope, timeline, and pricing within a few days.

How long does a project take?

A UX/CRO audit typically takes 2–4 weeks. A landing page redesign can be live within a week. Full website and shop builds run 4–8 weeks depending on scope. I'll give you a realistic timeline in the proposal — and flag it early if anything shifts.

What platforms do you work with?

Webflow, Shopify, Framer, WordPress, and Squarespace. I'll recommend the platform that fits your team's technical comfort and long-term needs — not the one I prefer.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. Audits start from a fixed price; design and build projects are scoped individually. You'll always get a clear quote before we start. Payment is split into three milestones: 30% upfront, 40% at interim review, 30% on delivery.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm based in Berlin and work with clients internationally. Communication runs through email, video calls, and shared Figma files. Timezone overlaps have never been a problem.

Who do you work best with?

E-commerce, wellness, and SaaS brands that already have traffic or a product — but suspect their website isn't converting the way it should. If you're past the "just need a website" stage and into "need the website to perform," we'll get along.

What happens after launch?

I hand over everything your team needs to maintain the site independently — documentation, logins, and a walkthrough if needed. If you want ongoing optimisation or A/B testing support, we can set that up too.
