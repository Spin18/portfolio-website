About

Hi, I'm Imen.

I'm a digital product consultant based in Berlin, originally from Monastir. I've spent 10+ years moving between corporate innovation labs (Deloitte, Siemens, BMW) and scrappy freelance builds, which means I know how to make big-brand thinking work on a startup budget.

I studied IT & Electrical Engineering at TU Munich, trained in neuromarketing at the Deloitte Neuroscience Institute, and now spend my days auditing, designing, and building websites that don't just look good — they perform.

Fluent in German, English, French and Arabic, and always up for a project that lets me build something a little different.
